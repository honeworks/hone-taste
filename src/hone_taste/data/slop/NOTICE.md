# Slop word and trigram lists

`slop_list.json` and `slop_list_trigrams.json` are copied unchanged from
[sam-paech/slop-score](https://github.com/sam-paech/slop-score) (`data/`, commit 289264ab2a0d, fetched 2026-09-27).

**Whose taste:** words and trigrams that LLMs use far more often than human writers, measured by the
upstream author on creative-writing and essay outputs of many models against a human-writing profile.
They are tuned for fiction and essays and include many fantasy names, so they can under-report in other
domains and flag some ordinary words.
